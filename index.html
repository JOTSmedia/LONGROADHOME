THE FILM

Going home.
Getting lost.

Strangers. Mirrors. Forests. A graveyard. Along a road that seems to circle back on itself, a man’s trip home becomes something far less certain.

Long Road Home moves between psychological mystery, dark humor, musical passages, and surreal encounters. Its monochrome and heightened color reveal a world where memory and identity refuse to stand still.
