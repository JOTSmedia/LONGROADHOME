THE STORY

The road is only
the beginning.

Genre
Dark comedy / Drama
Running time
89 minutes
Language
English

He sets out to find his way home. The people he meets have other things to teach him.

A quirky loner loses his way on a trip home, finding himself in a series of increasingly strange encounters. On the road, in the woods, and across diner tables, conversations take unexpected turns.

With dark humor and moments of surreal beauty, Long Road Home follows a journey through questions of life, death, and the difficulty of knowing yourself.

Fellow travelers.
